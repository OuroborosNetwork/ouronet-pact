# IGNIS cost inventory — #76 prep (static, decision-support)

Groundwork for **#76 (re-price IGNIS so cost reflects computational complexity)**. Generated by
`REPL/_ignis_cost_inventory.py` (scratch). Purpose: give an objective, whole-codebase view of what
every client/admin **core** entrypoint charges vs how heavy it actually is, so the pricing model can
be set by review-and-adjust rather than from scratch.

## Headline findings

1. **The cost model is non-uniform.** Only the **AQP family** (FVT/RPS/DSA/AQP-POOL/SCORE/ANK/VCT)
   charges a flat named `GAS|<OP>` base constant. Every other module (DPTF/DPOF/ATS/SWP/DPDC/DALOS/…,
   459 of 487 ops) computes its charge from a **`UsagePrice` tier + URCi_ formula** — shown as
   `computed` below (drill into that op's `URCi_<op>` to see the tier).
2. **AQP flat costs don't track complexity.** The `GAS|` bases are almost all **500 or 1000**
   regardless of the op's real work. Spot examples (see FVT table): `C_Issue` = 1000 with 4 cross-module
   refs, but `C_AddScoreEntity` = 500 with **12 refs + 2 reads** — the heavier op is priced lower.
   The sync ops are 50 (`GAS|SYNC-*`), the only sub-500 tier.
3. **Heavy (doubled `CC_`/`AA_`) + `defpact` ops** (flagged) reach `URH_/URHC_/URD_` scans — these are
   the genuinely expensive paths and the first place to check that price ≥ work.

## How to read the per-op table
`cost` = the `GAS|` base charged (resolved 1-level transitively through the op's `XI_`/`URCi_`), or
`computed` (UsagePrice/URCi formula). Complexity signals are from the op's **own body**: `writes`
(insert/update/write), `heavy-rd` (URH_/URHC_/URD_), `reads` (UR_/URC_), `XI/XE` (writer calls),
`ref::` (cross-module fan-out). A low `cost` next to high `writes`/`heavy-rd`/`ref::` = a re-price candidate.
