# Dead generators, and the artefacts they stopped guarding

*2026-09-15 — closing GS-01 / GS-02 / GS-03, and what was underneath them.*

## The shape of it

Three "defects in the generated pricing artefacts" had sat open since 2026-09-14. All three were
symptoms of one cause nobody had looked for: **both generators were dead.**

The 2026-09-14 tools move (`REPL/*.py` → `REPL/tools/*.py`) left eleven tools resolving a sibling
by a hard-coded old path. They raised `FileNotFoundError` **at module level, before argv was read**
— unconditionally dead, whatever the arguments. The move's commit message says *"verified by output
diffing"*, and that was true of the tools that were run. **A dead tool produces no output to diff.**

The artefacts were then **hand-edited while dead**: their `Generated by` and `Regenerate:` lines were
updated to the new `REPL/tools/...` paths. The provenance line of a document that could not be
regenerated was corrected to name a command that crashed. Meanwhile the worksheet still carried
`C_WipeHeavy`, `A_RemoveSecondary`, `C_BreakSemiFungibleSet` — **stale by the X-04 rename, 126
replacements across 30 files** — under a header asserting the two sheets *"cannot drift"*.

## What the three defects actually were

* **GS-01** was not a bug about literals. It was the walk **stopping one hop short of the cost**, so
  the only number left in range was a literal. `RT-F-001`'s refactor moved the charge behind a
  `URCi_` reader the walker *can* follow, so GS-01's own rows corrected themselves the moment the
  generator ran again.
* **GS-02** was recorded as "one entrypoint unpriced". It was **18**. And the mechanism was worse
  than the count: a `skipped` counter incremented at two sites and **printed nowhere**, under a
  footer reading `0 unresolved`.
* **GS-03** was a header that **retyped** numbers the body computes.

## Three new ones, found only because the generator could run

* **GS-10** — the walker was blind to three whole name classes: `X[IEB]v_` (the `v` variant
  prefix), `UDCx_*Cumulator`, and cost readers whose name **ends in `Ignis`**. The ATS/AQP stake
  family was therefore published at a floor of **3–6 IGNIS while the chain charges at least 18–21**.
* **GS-11** — the `RT-F-001` split was **double-counted**: the text carries the constant, the deter
  key, *and* a subtraction of the constant, and only the additive legs were summed.
* Four sites still charged a bare `100.0` instead of `LQ|INITIATION-FEE`.

## Rules worth keeping

**A coverage number that counts only what it managed to look at is worse than no number.** `skipped`
was write-only, so the sheet's own tally could never fall below "complete".

**Verification that only covers what ran does not cover what stopped running.** Output diffing
cannot see a tool that produces no output. The check has to be *"does it still execute"*, separately
from *"does it still agree"*.

**Retyping a number into prose creates a second source of truth that only ever loses.** The bucket
header is now derived from `_bucket` itself, boundaries discovered by probing.

**Reviving a dead mutator is not neutral.** Five of the eleven rewrite source at import with no
`__main__` guard. Immediately after fixing their paths, a loop that imported each one *"just to prove
it loads"* fired all five and silently deleted 111 lines of schemas from `05_FVT.pact`. A one-shot
migration script does not stop being a weapon because its job is finished — it stops being a weapon
when it refuses to fire. All five now require `--apply`.

**A checker that resolves drift by overwriting it is a green light wired to nothing.**
`_pricesync.py`'s first `--check` dispatch passed `write=True`, so "verify" silently rewrote the
artefacts and always reported success. The first full gate run after wiring it in was therefore
green on a check that could not fail.

## The guard

`REPL/tools/_pricesync.py --check`, fatal in `_gate.py`, regenerates both artefacts and diffs them
against the committed files, *before* the 86-entrypoint run. Proven by re-injecting GS-03 into the
worksheet: the gate exited 1 and named the line.
