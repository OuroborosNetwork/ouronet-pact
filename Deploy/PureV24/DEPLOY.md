## What it is — six entrypoints that shipped without a price

PureV23 added the Equity company controls to Talos and left their `INFO_` cost previews for
later. That is the one thing a client needs before it can let anyone press the button.

An entrypoint with no preview is an operation a wallet **cannot quote**. It is the same shape as
the dead INFO on Activate Standard Account, where the UI had nothing to show and a broken
operation was indistinguishable from an expensive one. Both the repo gate and
`@ouronet/talos-registry`'s own test enforce the pairing, and both went red the moment V23's
surface was recorded — **which is how this was found, after the deploy rather than before.** The
two rounds should have travelled together.

Rotation charges nothing because it calls no billing client — the preview says so rather than
inventing a figure. Branding is STOA-priced, matching the existing `INFO_DPDC|UpgradeBranding`.

**The retirement preview quotes the HOLDER'S LEG, not the gross amount.** At a packaged fraction
K, retiring n million costs the retirer `n × (1,000,000 − K)` of their own shares and the escrow
the rest. A client showing the gross figure would refuse operations the chain accepts.

## One module, and no new readers in EQUITY

The cost is composed from the sub-ops these entrypoints already perform —
`DPDC-MNG::URCi_AddQuantity` / `URCi_BurnSFT` / `URCi_TogglePause`,
`DPDC::URCi_UpdatePendingBranding` / `URCi_UpgradeBranding`, and
`DPDC-T::URCi_MultiTransferCumulator` for the transfer leg. Every one is already published in its
own interface.

Adding `URCi_` readers to EQUITY instead would have meant **`EquityV5` → `V6` and a second
cascade through five modules**, to restate a sum already available here.

**No interface bump.** `InfoTwoV3` declares 14 of INFO-TWO's many `INFO_` readers and these six
are not among them — module-only, like most of the file. Verified in the emitted file: 1
`(module`, **0 `(interface`**, 0 live `create-table`.
