# TS01-C1 — Stage-1 client orchestration — accounts and fungibles

## What it is for

The client surface for accounts and true fungibles: deploying accounts, rotating guards, issuing and moving tokens, roles, fees, freezing and wiping.

Talos is **the only supported client path**. Core modules build an unfinished bill that only Talos may settle, and the gas station sponsors **only** shapes it recognises. A core client function called directly would not have its gas paid and could not collect.

## Where it sits

Above the account, gas and true-fungible cores.

## Traps

**A client function cannot be called from inside its own module.** It builds a bill only Talos may collect, so a self-call can drop or double it.

**Sponsorship depends on what the transaction contains, not who sends it.** The gas station reads the transaction's code and accepts three shapes.

**Preview and charge call the same cost function**, so a quoted price cannot drift from the charged one. But the preview's parameter list usually differs from the operation's — 414 of 427 do. Bind by name.

**Account creation is priced in native currency and switched off.** The toggle is deliberately independent of every other fee, so onboarding stays free while the protocol charges for everything else.
