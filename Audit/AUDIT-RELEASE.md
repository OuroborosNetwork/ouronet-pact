## What changed in 2.0

- PATRON/EXECUTOR SEPARATION, the whole AQP family. 52 of the 89 patron-taking C_/A_ entrypoints now name the account that ACTS separately from the account that PAYS. Before this, an entrypoint took a `patron` and the actor was either that same account or a value derived inside a capability and never named -- so 'who is doing this?' had no answer at the call site, and several functions answered it WRONGLY. Every edit keeps the existing derived ownership gate and adds the executor check BESIDE it; the derived check was never replaced, which is the single edit that would have turned this into an authorisation hole.
- SECURITY: the two DPNF anchor paths (ANK|C>ISSUE-DPNF, -DPNF-SET) enforced NO ownership at all, while their DPTF sibling ran CAP_TF|Owner and their DPSF sibling ran CAP_OwnerOrCreator. UEV_AssetAnchorCap caps an asset at 49 anchors, so a stranger could mint 49 anchors against a collection he did not own, exhaust the cap PERMANENTLY, and own every boost class created along the way. It was being exercised: closing the gate immediately failed two fixtures and the BOOT itself, all anchoring collections owned by KST.LUMY while signing only as the admin.
- SECURITY: C_IssueSingleScoreModel and C_CombineTripletScoreModel enforced only 'the patron account exists' -- nothing asserted the caller owned it. Not exploitable, because the Talos wrappers do reach a patron ownership enforce and P|UEV_IMC makes Talos the only reachable path; the hole was one layer of defence deep, not zero. Closed anyway, and closing it is what now lets a sponsor pay for someone else's model. C_IssueMultipletFamily had the same shape and the same fix.
- DEFECT (state-corrupting): C_AdmitAgency passed `patron` as the OPERATOR in four places, one of them WRITTEN TO STATE as DSA|Agency.operator-konto. A sponsored agency open would have recorded the sponsor -- or, under the gasless patron, DALOS|SC_NAME -- as the agency's operator permanently. Every other defect in this release is recoverable by calling again with the right account; this one wrote the wrong owner into a row.
- DEFECT: the boost-class owner added in 1.1 was being filled with the PATRON at all four inline-creation sites, so under a gasless patron every inline boost class would have been owned by DALOS|SC_NAME rather than its creator -- silently, since nothing reads it back at issuance.
- DEFECT: six DSA entrypoints ran `(enforce (= patron fvt-owner))`, forcing the GAS PAYER to BE the FVT owner. No sponsor could pay for a vault owner's operation and the gasless patron was unusable across that entire surface.
- DEFECT: MTX|2|C_Inject hardcoded `XB_FvtInject patron patron`, so the documented spike fallback for the Stage Two daily emission could only run when the gas payer was also the token source -- which is precisely the shape it exists to rescue. The minter's own @doc asserted the fallback was available; that assertion was false.
- INSTRUMENT: ARCHITECTURE/AUTH-SURFACE.md records, per entrypoint, every ownership enforce reachable transitively through its capability chain -- 1,104 entrypoints, 813 enforcing. _authsurface.py --check is FATAL in the gate and fails if any entrypoint's set shrinks. As far as we can tell this is the first time the system's authorisation surface has been written down. It reported 'none weakened, N strengthened' on every commit in this release, which is how the closed holes were counted rather than claimed.
- The Stage Two daily emission (AA_OuroMinterStageTwo) was measured for the first time: ~940,000 gas, roughly HALF a 2M block, against Stage One's ~135,000. The figure is a FLOOR, not a ceiling -- four of its six legs scale with staker count. Pinned as a band rather than a number, because a regression that silently skips legs makes the emission CHEAPER and without a lower bound that reads as an improvement.
